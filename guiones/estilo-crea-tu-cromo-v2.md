# Prompt de estilo v2 — Crea tu cromo

> Describe el estilo de las imágenes elegidas en `arte/v2/`. Usar este para todo lo nuevo, así combina con lo que ya está en la herramienta.
> Pegar en el campo de estilo de Storyboard Studio o al principio del guion. Las tintas de cada cuadro van en el texto de acción, no en notas `>`.

```
VISUAL STYLE FOR EVERY FRAME

Nocturnal illustrated storybook. Hand-drawn ink linework with loose cross-hatching and visible pen strokes, over rich textured colour with fine print grain, like a lithograph or a screenprinted book plate. Everything sits in deep night: near-black and dark navy shadows, with warm amber candle or lamp light as the main light source.

Each frame has one dominant saturated accent colour that belongs to that scene (teal, crimson, olive, emerald, plum, vermilion, mustard, violet), carried by a large object or surface: a cloth, a sky, a banner, a flame. The rest stays dark and warm.

Magical moments and isolated objects are shown inside layered cut-paper frames: several stepped paper layers in dark tones around the object, with light glowing between them and stippled sparks drifting out.

Composition: an object close to the camera crowds one edge of the frame and is cropped by it (a curtain, a bedpost, a table edge, a hat brim, a boat prow). The main subject sits off-centre and some dark empty space is left for text.

Point of view: first person. No people, no faces, no hands, no figures, no silhouettes of people, no crowds.

Never: text, letters, writing, numbers, scribbles that look like handwriting, logos, crests, shields, coats of arms, emblems on banners. Never photorealism, 3D render, glossy digital painting or cinematic film look. Do not reproduce designs from the Harry Potter films: castles, trains, hats, signs and rooms are original designs described by the script.
```

## Resumen en castellano

- **Libro ilustrado nocturno:** tinta con rayado suelto sobre color con grano de impresión. Sombras negras y azul noche, luz cálida de vela.
- **Un color fuerte por cuadro**, puesto en un objeto grande (paño, cielo, estandarte, llama). Lo dice el texto de acción.
- **Papel recortado en capas** alrededor de los objetos mágicos y en los destellos.
- **Un objeto cercano invade un borde**, el tema principal va descentrado y queda espacio oscuro para el texto.
- **Sin personas, sin escritura, sin escudos, sin diseños de las películas.**
